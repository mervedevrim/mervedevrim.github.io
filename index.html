About

Since March 2021, I've worked remotely as a webmaster for a web design and development studio, maintaining client websites. The work spans many sites and several systems: WordPress, in-house CMS platforms, the admin panels of PHP-based websites, and static sites I edit directly through FTP.

I handle the full update cycle: content and page changes, HTML/CSS edits, web visuals in Photoshop, email newsletters, and client requests through our helpdesk. Alongside this, I manage a WordPress and WooCommerce website on a freelance basis.

Before working on the web, I spent years in data quality control and graphic design. That background still shows in how I work: careful with details and comfortable with visuals.
